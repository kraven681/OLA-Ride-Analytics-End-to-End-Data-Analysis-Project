# Ola Ride Booking Analysis Dashboard

A complete Power BI dashboard for analyzing Ola ride booking
performance, revenue, cancellations, vehicle wise metrics, and customer
ratings.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Ola ride booking data and converts raw records
into an interactive business intelligence dashboard using SQL and Power
BI. The dashboard helps business teams monitor ride performance,
identify revenue trends, understand cancellation reasons, compare
vehicle categories, and track customer satisfaction through a single
reporting solution.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow. Data extraction, SQL
analysis, data modeling, DAX calculations, dashboard design, and
business insight generation.

## Problem Statement

Ride booking platforms generate thousands of records every day. Raw
transactional data does not help business teams make quick decisions.

This project solves the following business problems.

-   Monitor total bookings and booking value.
-   Track successful and cancelled rides.
-   Identify customer and driver cancellation reasons.
-   Compare vehicle categories using revenue and distance metrics.
-   Measure customer and driver ratings.
-   Analyze payment method contribution.
-   Observe booking trends over time.
-   Support data driven business decisions through interactive
    dashboards.

-   Total Bookings: 103,024
-   Total Booking Value: 35.08 Million
-   Successful Bookings: 63,967
-   Cancellation Rate: 28.08%
-   Cash generated the highest revenue.
-   Prime Sedan generated the highest booking value.
-   Ratings remained close to 4.0 across vehicle types.
